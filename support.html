Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with CuraMate?

Download the app from the App Store, create your account, and complete your health profile. You can then start chatting with AI doctors, tracking your health, and receiving personalized recommendations.

Is CuraMate a replacement for medical professionals?

No, CuraMate is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical concerns. CuraMate provides informational and educational content to support your health journey.

How does the AI doctor chat work?

Our AI doctors use advanced language models to provide health information and guidance. Simply type your health questions or concerns, and receive personalized responses based on your profile and health data.

Can I upload images for analysis?

Yes! You can upload photos of symptoms, skin conditions, or other health concerns. Our AI will analyze the images and provide insights. Remember, this is for informational purposes only and not a medical diagnosis.

How do I track my health data?

CuraMate allows you to track daily habits, wellness points, active streaks, and more. The app integrates with Apple Health to sync your health data automatically (with your permission).

What are wellness points and streaks?

Wellness points are earned by completing healthy habits and activities. Active streaks track consecutive days of engagement. Both help motivate you to maintain healthy routines.

How do I manage my subscription?

You can manage your subscription through your device settings. Go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions, then select CuraMate to view, modify, or cancel your subscription.

Is my health data secure?

Yes, we take your privacy and data security seriously. All health data is encrypted and stored securely. Please review our Privacy Policy for detailed information about how we protect your data.

How do I reset my password?

If you need to reset your password, use the "Forgot Password" option on the login screen. You'll receive instructions via email to reset your password securely.

Quick Help

📱 App Issues

Experiencing crashes or bugs? Try restarting the app or updating to the latest version from the App Store.

🔔 Notifications

Not receiving reminders? Check your device notification settings and ensure CuraMate has permission to send notifications.

💾 Data Sync

Health data not syncing? Verify that you've granted CuraMate permission to access Apple Health in your device settings.

💳 Billing

Questions about charges or refunds? Contact us at support@curamate.com or manage subscriptions through your device settings.
